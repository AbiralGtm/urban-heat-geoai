Kathmandu urban heat

Pre-monsoon daytime surface temperature, 15 March to 31 May 2024, averaged over H3 hexagons of about 0.74 km².

Surface temperature from Landsat 8/9 at about 10:30 local time. It is not air temperature. The exposure layer is heat times population density; it shows how many people live where it is hot, not who is harmed.
